## Key findings

- **The market has split into two price tiers, roughly ₹36,000 apart.** This gap is far too large to be ordinary discounting.
  - **Marketplace tier — ₹83,000 to ₹93,000:** Flipkart and Amazon.
  - **Authorised tier — ₹98,000 to ₹1,20,000:** Samsung's own store, Vijay Sales, Poorvika, Reliance Digital.
- **Both Flipkart listings are sold by NexaPhones, a third-party seller — not a Samsung-authorised channel.**
- **Authorised supply appears to be drying up.** Vijay Sales is pre-book only, Poorvika is out of stock, and Samsung's own store has colours unavailable — consistent with an 18-month-old flagship nearing end of life.
- On the verified data, **Flipkart (Titanium Gray) is the cheapest at ₹83,053**. See the caveat below before acting on that.
- **Amazon's ₹92,999 is close to its all-time low** of ₹92,900 (recorded 30 May 2026), against an average of ₹1,07,033.

- **Samsung India:** ₹12,000 instant bank discount on all leading banks; Samsung Assured Buyback up to 70% resale value; Samsung Care+ and Finance+ EMI.
- **Vijay Sales:** free Portronics Sound Slick V soundbar (stated value ₹3,199); 900 loyalty points.

---

## Recommendation

- **Cheapest verified price: Flipkart, Titanium Gray, at ₹83,053 effective.**
- **However, this should not be read as "best buy" without checking the seller.** A discount of roughly 30% from a third-party marketplace seller, at a time when every authorised channel holds near MRP, is a well-known pattern for **grey-market or imported units**, which typically carry no valid Indian warranty. This could not be confirmed or ruled out from the listing itself.
- **For a guaranteed Indian warranty**, the safest verified option is **Samsung India at approximately ₹97,999** (inclusive of the ₹12,000 bank discount) — about ₹15,000 more than Flipkart.
- **Suggested action before purchase:** confirm NexaPhones' warranty terms on Flipkart, and verify Amazon's live price directly.

## Caveats

- **The MRP is inconsistent across platforms.** Flipkart and Vijay Sales quote ₹1,29,999 (the January 2025 launch price); Samsung, Reliance, and Poorvika quote ₹1,19,999 (an apparently revised MRP). Flipkart's headline "33% off" is calculated against the higher figure, which flatters the discount.
- **Colour variants differ between platforms.** The two Flipkart rows are different colours and differ by ₹1,520, so colour materially affects price.
- **Samsung's ₹97,999 is not strictly like-for-like** with the other rows, since it already includes a bank discount.
- **Prices and bank offers change daily.** This snapshot is valid for **14 July 2026** only.
